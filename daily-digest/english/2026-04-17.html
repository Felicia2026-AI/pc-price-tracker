1. Reading Passage
Supply Chain Cost Analysis: Memory Price Surge Impact

Good morning, team. I want to share our latest analysis on how the memory price surge is impacting our parts cost structure.

As you know, DRAM contract prices are expected to jump 58-63% in Q2, while NAND flash could see increases of 70-75%. This puts significant pressure on our net parts cost, especially for models with high memory configurations.

However, we're not sitting idle. We've been working with our suppliers to lock in contracts ahead of the price increases, which has helped us mitigate roughly 40% of the exposure. For Q3, we're exploring alternative suppliers and monitoring the spot market closely, since consumer-grade memory prices have started to pull back.

Looking at the regional breakdown, our China-based repair operations still show a cost advantage of about 15-20% compared to US repairs, even accounting for the recent tariff adjustments. I'd recommend we continue prioritizing China for standard repairs while reserving US facilities for high-priority cases.

Questions?
2. 中文译文
供应链成本分析：内存价格上涨影响

早上好，团队。我想和大家分享我们关于内存价格上涨如何影响备件成本结构的最新分析。

众所周知，DRAM合约价预计在Q2上涨58-63%，而NAND闪存涨幅可能达到70-75%。这给我们的净备件成本带来了显著压力，尤其是高内存配置的机型。

然而，我们并没有坐以待毙。我们一直与供应商合作，在价格上涨之前锁定合约，这帮助我们对冲了约40%的敞口。对于Q3，我们正在探索替代供应商，并密切关注现货市场，因为消费级内存价格已开始回落。

从区域拆解来看，即使考虑到最近的关税调整，我们的国内维修运营仍比美国维修点保持约15-20%的成本优势。我建议我们继续优先选择国内进行标准维修，同时保留美国设施处理高优先级案例。

有什么问题吗？
3. Key Vocabulary & Expressions
  • jump (v.)
    / dʒʌmp /
    大幅上涨/跃升；比 rise/increase 更强调陡然变化
    "DRAM prices are expected to jump 58-63% in Q2."
    预计Q2 DRAM价格将大涨58-63%。
  • put pressure on (sth)
    对...造成压力；比 "affect" 更强调被动承压感
    "This puts significant pressure on our net parts cost."
    这给我们的净备件成本带来显著压力。
  • lock in (a contract / pricing)
    锁定（合约/价格）；提前固定条件以规避未来风险
    "We've been working to lock in contracts ahead of price increases."
    我们一直在争取在价格上涨前锁定合约。
  • mitigate exposure
    降低敞口/减少风险敞口；mitigate = 减轻，exposure = 风险敞口
    "This helped us mitigate roughly 40% of the exposure."
    这帮助我们降低了约40%的风险敞口。
  • pull back
    回落/回调；形容价格从高点向下调整
    "Consumer-grade memory prices have started to pull back."
    消费级内存价格已开始回落。
  • cost advantage of (X%)
    保持（X%）的成本优势；正式汇报中常用
    "Our China operations show a cost advantage of about 15-20%."
    我们的国内运营保持约15-20%的成本优势。
💡 语言小贴士："jump" vs "rise"：在描述价格大幅波动时，jump 更能传达陡然变化的感觉，比 rise 更口语化且有力度。在成本汇报中善用 "mitigate"（减轻）和 "exposure"（敞口）这两个词，显得更专业。
