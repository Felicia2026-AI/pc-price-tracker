📖 English Passage

Supply Chain Cost Management in the PC Industry

Effective supply chain cost management is critical for maintaining profitability in the competitive PC industry. Leading manufacturers are increasingly focusing on SOID-level cost analysis to gain granular visibility into their parts consumption and recovery processes.

By tracking net parts cost — calculated as consumption minus credit plus repair expenses — companies can identify cost optimization opportunities across different geographic regions and component types. This data-driven approach enables more accurate forecasting and better decision-making in procurement and inventory management.

Key metrics include repair rates, yield rates, and credit recovery rates, which help organizations understand the efficiency of their reverse logistics operations. Regional comparisons, particularly between China and international repair facilities, provide insights into cost structure differences and potential process improvements.

🔑 Key Vocabulary

  • Granular /ˈɡrænjələr/
    adj. 详细的，细粒度的
    We need more granular data to understand the cost drivers at the component level.
  • Procurement /prəˈkjʊərmənt/
    n. 采购，获得
    The procurement team negotiated better prices with our suppliers.
  • Visibility /ˌvɪzəˈbɪləti/
    n. 可见性，透明度
    We need better visibility into our supply chain to reduce risks.
  • Reverse logistics /rɪˈvɜːrs ləˈdʒɪstɪks/
    n. 逆向物流（退货、维修、回收等）
    Our reverse logistics operations have improved significantly this quarter.
  • Yield rate /jiːld reɪt/
    n. 良品率，收益率
    The yield rate for our repair process increased from 85% to 92%.

💡 Chinese Translation

PC行业供应链成本管理

在竞争激烈的PC行业中，有效的供应链成本管理对于维持盈利能力至关重要。领先的制造商越来越关注SOID级别的成本分析，以获得对零部件消耗和回收过程的详细可见性。

通过追踪净零部件成本——计算公式为消耗减去回收加上维修费用——公司可以识别不同地理地区和零部件类型的成本优化机会。这种数据驱动的方法能够实现更准确的预测，并在采购和库存管理中做出更好的决策。

关键指标包括维修率、良品率和回收率，这些指标帮助组织了解其逆向物流运营的效率。区域比较，特别是中国与国际维修设施之间的比较，提供了成本结构差异和潜在流程改进的洞察。
