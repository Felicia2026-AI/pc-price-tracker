1. Reading Passage
Presenting Cost Analysis in a Business Review

Good morning, everyone. Thanks for joining today's review. I'd like to walk you through our Q1 cost performance for the service supply chain.

Overall, we are tracking slightly above target — about 3% over plan — mainly driven by a sharp spike in component costs. DRAM and NAND prices have surged significantly this quarter, which has pushed up our parts cost per unit.

That said, we've taken some proactive steps. We locked in contract pricing with two key suppliers before the market moved, which helped us offset roughly half of the exposure. Going forward, I'd recommend we keep a close eye on Q2 procurement windows, because memory prices are expected to stay elevated through the year.

On the positive side, our repair yield rate improved by 2 percentage points compared to last quarter, which helped bring down our net parts cost. I'll share the breakdown by region in the next slide.

Any questions before I move on?
2. 中文译文
在业务回顾会上呈现成本分析

大家早上好，感谢参加今天的回顾会。我来带大家看一下我们服务供应链Q1的成本表现。

总体来看，我们比目标略高——大约超出计划3%——主要驱动因素是零部件成本的大幅上涨。本季度DRAM和NAND价格明显飙升，推高了每台的备件成本。

尽管如此，我们提前采取了一些措施。在市场波动之前，我们与两家关键供应商锁定了合约价格，这帮助我们对冲了约一半的敞口。展望未来，我建议我们密切关注Q2的采购时间窗口，因为内存价格预计全年都将维持在高位。

积极的一面是，我们的维修良率比上季度提升了2个百分点，有助于降低净备件成本。我将在下一张幻灯片中按地区详细拆解。

在我继续之前，有什么问题吗？
3. Key Vocabulary & Expressions
  • walk you through (sth)
    / wɔːk juː θruː /
    带大家逐步过一遍；详细讲解某事
    "Let me walk you through our Q1 cost breakdown."
    我来带大家过一下我们Q1的成本拆解。
  • tracking above / below target
    （实际进展）高于 / 低于目标；常用于汇报数据
    "We are tracking 3% above plan this quarter."
    本季度我们超出计划3%。
  • lock in (pricing / a deal)
    锁定（价格 / 合同）；提前固定条件以规避风险
    "We locked in contract pricing before the market moved."
    我们在市场波动之前锁定了合约价格。
  • offset exposure
    对冲风险敞口；用某一动作来抵消潜在损失
    "The early contracts helped offset roughly half of our cost exposure."
    提前签的合同帮助我们对冲了约一半的成本敞口。
  • going forward
    展望未来；从现在起（正式汇报常用过渡语）
    "Going forward, I'd recommend we keep a close eye on procurement windows."
    展望未来，我建议我们密切关注采购时间窗口。
  • yield rate
    良率（指维修/生产合格率）；与 repair yield 连用
    "Our repair yield rate improved by 2 percentage points."
    我们的维修良率提升了2个百分点。
💡 语言小贴士：汇报数据时，"tracking above/below"比 "higher/lower than" 更地道，也更客观。搭配 "roughly"（大约）使数据表达留有余地，是高级职场表达的标志。
4. Understanding Check
Q1. According to the passage, why is the company tracking above target?
A. Because repair yield rates declined
B. Because component costs surged sharply
C. Because the team missed procurement windows
D. Because suppliers raised shipping fees
✅ Answer: B — "mainly driven by a sharp spike in component costs"
Q2. What proactive step did the team take to manage cost risk?
A. They reduced repair volume
B. They switched to new suppliers
C. They locked in contract pricing before the market moved
D. They delayed Q2 procurement
✅ Answer: C — "We locked in contract pricing with two key suppliers before the market moved"
Q3. 翻译成英文：我们的净备件成本比上季度下降了5%，主要得益于良率提升。
✅ 参考答案：Our net parts cost declined by 5% compared to last quarter, mainly driven by an improvement in repair yield rate.
